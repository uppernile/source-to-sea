About

Upper Nile
Collection

Upper Nile Collection is a small group of privately chartered dahabiyas sailing the stretch of river between Aswan and Esna. Each boat is built and maintained in Esna by the boatwrights who have worked this water for generations.

Philosophy, provenance, restoration and the people behind the collection will live in this section — away from the river, where a reader can slow down.
